    How the meal library works

    When you click Generate plan for a client, the app builds the plan from these meals. For each day it picks one meal per slot (breakfast, snacks, lunch, dinner) and:

    • skips meals that contain the client's allergies or dislikes (it checks every food name),
    • skips meals tagged salty for high blood pressure and sugary for diabetes, and uses only vegetarian/vegan meals when the client's notes say so,
    • doesn't repeat a meal on back-to-back days, and prefers meals whose protein fits the target,
    • scales portions so each meal gets its share of the day's calories.

    The more meals you add, the more varied the plans. Write the grams for one normal portion; the planner scales them. Each food needs a USDA search term in English (e.g. "chicken breast cooked roasted"). Check the match shown under it.
